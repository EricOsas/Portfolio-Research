# NQ Precision

The source layout matches VaultBreak, OvernightBiasRangeBreakout and VWAPPullback: one thin `NQ Precision Bot.mq5` entry file, `StrategyEngine.mqh`, `ExecutionRuntime.mqh`, and `ChartVisuals.mqh`. The runtime and chart framework come from the installed portfolio EAs. Risk controls, automatic broker clock, ownership journal, timer, close reconciliation, logging and panel follow that structure. Only MetaTrader's standard Trade library is needed beyond these root-local files; EX5 runs alone.

The signal engine uses completed M1 bars regardless of the chart timeframe. Wilder ATR14/50 and inclusive RVOL14 feed a 09:30–09:45 Eastern range and first available tick at/after09:59:55. One decision is consumed per day; signal-anchored brackets default to2.2/0.3ATR, with an eight-minute time close from the actual fill. Periods, filter bands, range, trigger and time exit are exposed for separately named research settings. Range coverage follows the selected window length, including shorter ranges.

`SignalData=NativeChartData` follows the symbol's own chart mode: Bid-price/tick-volume for broker CFDs, Last-price/real-volume for futures. Explicit overrides are available. `InpResearchUTC=false` uses the portfolio automatic clock: live server/GMT or tester weekly-opening anchor. The strict imported NQ preset sets UTC research mode and Last/real-volume explicitly. Do not compare a USTEC lot, CFD tick volume or quote-side fill directly with an NQ contract and Last tape.

In Last/real-volume mode, only actual trade events (Last, Volume, Buy or Sell tick flags) can advance the trigger, queued entry or time close. Quote-only callbacks may carry a cached Last; they still update safety/history but cannot manufacture a trade event. Bid/CFD mode continues processing quote callbacks. This repair was compiled without opening a separate terminal profile; full native parity remains a separate result gate.

M1 OHLC, generated Every Tick and Real Ticks all operate on their available ticks. Coarse modes can encounter the fixed trigger later than09:59:55 and produce different trades and fills; no precise intraminute path is invented. The default risk controls match the portfolio EAs: cash risk100 including modeled costs; fixed lots are optional. The NQ research preset separately sets six contracts and zero modeled friction for gross same-tape comparisons. Broker costs remain a separate validation dimension.

The fixed magic identity remains1095958. Recovery uses the reference account/server/symbol/magic scope and verified position identity, preserves broker protection while evidence is unresolved, and holds pending closes instead of ordinary account-wide action. Live operation inherits the reference hedging-account requirement. Native builds, calculation fixtures and historical tester results do not establish VPS restart, disconnect or broker runtime acceptance. Do not activate a new build over an unresolved position journal.

The 1.21 safety pass follows RBE, TokyoCoil, TuesdayTurnaround and OpenDrive. Close/modify permission requires the exact restored position identifier and opening time, or a verified current entry result; symbol/magic alone does not authorize a startup timer close. Original time-close settings/deadline are checkpointed before entry and recovered for the active trade. A legacy active position without that contract holds with its broker protection intact. Original risk, current broker SL/TP and cost/management state remain those of the actual owned position.

Close requests commit a millisecond timestamp and exact position/request quantity before sending, retain unresolved acknowledgements through restart, and cannot retire from an earlier partial exit within the same second. Timer retries handle missing entry/history evidence and close reconciliation without requiring ticks. Corrupt records and identity conflicts remain holds. Normal time exits are queued on admissible ticks, not initiated by a wall-clock timer.

Broker trade-disabled, server/client-disable, close-only, long/short-only and permission/restriction responses are persisted in an account/server/symbol/magic-scoped restriction latch before normal rejection handling. The latch is independent of daily state; entries, closes and modifications pause while existing broker protection remains. Review broker status and exact owned position/protection before administrative recovery; no automatic daily reset clears a restriction.

The OpenDrive visible-exposure guard allows same-direction foreign sleeves. It always rejects opposing exact-symbol positions/pending entries; `BlockOpposingExposure=true` additionally checks the visible index family. It scans the current terminal's account state and is not an atomic reservation protocol across unrelated EAs or terminals. Do not infer cross-terminal race prevention from the local pending-request journal.

Trade/decision logs use the platform's Common/Files sandbox under `NQPrecision/logs`. These files are runtime data, not source dependencies. The recovery requirements above remain part of this root-local deployment. Prior source snapshots and the parity evidence are retained in the Codex task outputs.

## Standalone VPS source and execution visuals

Version 1.24 defaults `InpUseTickHistory=true`. Completed signal minutes, indicator warmup, ranges and historical visual replay are built from tick history under the same ETH minute contract as the Python/NT8 model. Trade mode requires actual Last prints and real volume; BidQuoteTickVolume uses broker Bid quotes and tick counts. Equal-millisecond records are retained; empty minutes are not invented. Setting the input false explicitly selects stored-M1 approximation, including synthetic tester OHLC modes, which cannot establish tick parity. Re-enable the new input when loading an older saved preset.

Tick warmup is incremental, bounded to four hourly chunks per update; missing history blocks new entries while existing close/protection processing remains active. The historical visual cache is capped at 10,000 completed minutes and two valid setups. Runtime ownership, durable pending requests, restart recovery and protection handling are unchanged. Raw-tick minute fixtures and ATR/RVOL fixtures match Python, and native compilation passes; these checks do not constitute a new full MT5 strategy fill backtest or equivalence between USTEC CFD quotes and CME NQ prints.

The complete strategy source is `NQ Precision Bot.mq5`, `StrategyEngine.mqh`, `ExecutionRuntime.mqh` and `ChartVisuals.mqh`, together in this folder. Only the standard MetaTrader `Trade/Trade.mqh` library is imported externally. No sibling EA, custom indicator, Python script or external source/config file is needed to compile. Use a normal MT5 installation with its standard library. Preserve existing terminal global-variable recovery state and runtime journals when moving an active deployment; source independence does not replace recovery data.

Execution rectangles are unfilled, with two-pixel borders: RBE/TokyoCoil gray risk, blue profit and light entry lines. They start at entry bar +2, follow open trades at max(entry +2, current viewed bar +1), and close at max(entry +2, actual exit bar). A resolved +2 trade shrinks back from its +3 live projection. Chart-timeframe changes reconstruct broker drawings from history. Range rectangles use the dark reference range color on the background layer, behind price and foreground execution/panel objects.

Range rectangles grow after each completed source M1 candle and stop at the configured range end. The visual cache retains at most two valid setup sessions; today's contiguous forming range counts as the newest once its first minute closes. A past range must have complete contiguous M1 coverage. The scan runs at most once per minute, stops after two valid sessions, and checks at most 32 calendar days if history is missing. Broker visual-history selection begins at the older retained range, and only trades from those two session dates are drawn. Old range, broker-box and per-deal objects are retired on window changes and rebuilt after timeframe changes. This display limit does not truncate order-management/recovery evidence.

Version 1.23 matches the original NT8 range-line horizon: trigger time (or TradeUntil in window mode) + InpCloseAfterMin + 2 drawing minutes. Defaults end at 10:09:55 New York, converted to broker time (17:09:55 with a +7-hour broker offset). The box retains its formation bounds. Automatic signal data uses Bid/tick volume for CFD calculation modes, including CFD indexes such as USTEC; explicit LastTradeRealVolume remains strict and requires actual Last prints and real volume. It does not synthesize futures volume from CFD quote counts.

Like RBE, the live chart can show historical simulated executions for the two retained setups when this EA has no matching broker deals. The replay reads at most 10,000 M1 bars for indicator warmup and fetches ticks in one-minute chunks from the setup trigger to resolution or 16:00 NY. It caches resolved/rejected setups, retries active/missing-data setups at most once per minute, uses only completed M1 filters, preserves next-event entry/time-close sequencing and the native absolute-bracket check, and never sends orders or modifies recovery state. Matching broker deals take precedence. Replay objects explicitly say simulated/history/no broker order; they are not account fills or proof of native/backtest parity. Missing tick history or a failed signal can legitimately leave no execution box. Unresolved historical replay projections stop at their session's data boundary rather than stretching into later sessions.
